{{ ui.icon('spark') }} How conversion works

  • "Login as Clerk" becomes a saved Clerk session, never a typed password.
  • "Open New Application" is matched to the screen with that title or link.
  • "Enter Mobile = 98765" fills the field labelled Mobile; "valid details" uses safe valid data.
  • "Error is shown" or "Status changes to Approved" become checks.
